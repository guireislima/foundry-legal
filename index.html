Digital Asset Foundry

An internal publishing tool for our own media brands.

Digital Asset Foundry is a private, single-operator application. It produces original short-form videos, images and posts (scripts, narration, editing and captions) and publishes them to social media accounts and channels that we own and operate, such as Spacesplained and Cataki Achados.

The application uses official platform APIs (for example the YouTube Data API, the TikTok Content Posting API, the Instagram Graph API and the Pinterest API) only to:

  • upload and publish content to our own accounts;
  • read basic performance metrics (views, likes, followers) of that same content, to decide what to make next;
  • reply to comments on our own videos.

It is not offered to the public, does not have end users other than its operator, and does not access other people's accounts or data.
