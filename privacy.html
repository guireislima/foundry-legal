Privacy Policy

Digital Asset Foundry · Last updated: November 15, 2026

Who we are

Digital Asset Foundry ("the app", "we") is a private application operated by a single operator to publish original content to social media accounts that the operator owns. Contact: foundrydigitalasset@gmail.com.

Whose data the app handles

The app is used only by its operator, with the operator's own accounts. It does not offer sign-in to the public and does not request access to anyone else's account.

What data we access

When the operator authorizes the app on a platform (Google/YouTube, TikTok, Meta/Instagram/Facebook, Pinterest), the app receives an access token for that account and uses it to:

  • upload videos, images and posts, and set their titles, descriptions and privacy status;
  • read the account's basic profile (account or channel ID and name) to know where it publishes;
  • read performance statistics of the content it published (views, likes, comments count, followers).

The app does not read private messages, contact lists, or personal data of viewers or other users.

How data is stored

Access tokens and statistics are stored locally on the operator's computer, in files that are not shared or published. Tokens are used only to call the official APIs of the platform that issued them.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data obtained through platform APIs with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept while the related content is online. Tokens are kept until the operator revokes access. Access can be revoked at any time from the platform's own settings, for example Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of any stored data, write to the contact address above; we delete it within 30 days.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
